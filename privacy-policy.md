# WizNomad: Travel Calculator — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information WizNomad: Travel Calculator ("WizNomad", "the app") uses, why, who receives it and the choices you have. WizNomad is made by **bitwigblazer**, the developer of WizNomad on Google Play ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the information described here, except where Google acts as an independent controller (see *Advertising*).

WizNomad is used around the world. We follow the strictest of the rules that apply to it, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and other US state privacy laws, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Japan's APPI, Singapore's PDPA, Australia's Privacy Act, South Korea's PIPA and India's DPDP Act. Where a law gives you more rights than this policy describes, you have those rights.

## In short

- **No account, no sign-in.** We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- **What you type stays on your phone.** Amounts, currencies, bills, cities and settings are saved only on your device.
- **Ads** are shown by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app.
- **Crash reports** help us fix bugs. They contain technical details about the crash, not what you typed.
- We **don't sell** your personal information for money, and we have no servers of our own that store it.

## 1. Information kept only on your phone

The app saves your choices so they are there next time: the currencies and their order in Convert, the last downloaded exchange rates, Tip & Split country settings, cities in Time zones, units, sizes, light/dark theme and similar settings. It also reads, on the phone only, the phone's language, region (to suggest your home currency and country) and time zone (to show the time where you are).

This information is **not sent to us**. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. If you use Android's backup, Android may include it in your own Google account backup, under Google's terms; you can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## 2. Exchange rates

To show current rates, the app downloads a public list of rates from **ExchangeRate-API** (exchangerate-api.com) about once a day. Like any internet request, this tells their server your device's IP address and basic technical details (for example, that the request came from an app). No information you typed is sent. See ExchangeRate-API's privacy policy: https://www.exchangerate-api.com/privacy

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by ads from **Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends to Google:

- your device's **advertising ID** and app set ID;
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not precise location);
- technical information such as device model, operating system, app version and language;
- interactions with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped), and diagnostic data about the ad SDK.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse. Ads may be **personalised** (based on your interests) only where you allow it; otherwise Google shows non-personalised or limited ads, which still use some information for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

**Your choices about ads**

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before any ad is requested, the app shows a consent message from Google (certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners.
- **US states with privacy laws (such as California):** you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the message the app shows.
- **Changing your choice:** wherever the app asks, you can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the app's menu (⋮ at the top of the screen) and tap **Privacy options**. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

For this processing, Google is an independent controller. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Legal basis (GDPR):** your consent for storing or reading information on your device and for personalised ads; our legitimate interest in funding a free app for non-personalised ads where the law allows it.

## 4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, **Firebase Crashlytics** (a Google service) sends us a crash report: the error and where in the code it happened, device model, operating system version, app version, the time, and a random installation identifier (not your name or advertising ID). We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. We don't use Google Analytics.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. See Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 5. Who receives information

Only the providers named above — Google (AdMob, Firebase) and ExchangeRate-API — for the purposes described. We don't sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, letting Google use information for personalised ads may count as "selling" or "sharing"; you can opt out as described in section 3. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Google and ExchangeRate-API may process information in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

## 7. How long information is kept

- On your phone: until you change it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- Crash reports: 90 days.
- Advertising data: as set out in Google's policies.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict its use; to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use); to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising; and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

Because we keep no account and no server copy of what you type, most information can be removed by you directly: clear the app's data or uninstall it, reset your advertising ID, and change your ad choices with **Privacy options** in the app's menu. For anything else, email us at **bitwigblazer@gmail.com**. We will reply within the time the law requires (usually 30 days). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children

WizNomad is a general travel tool and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age set by local law, such as 16 in some EU countries). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

## 10. Security

Information sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information on your phone is protected by Android's app storage, which other apps can't read.

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or on the app's store page.
